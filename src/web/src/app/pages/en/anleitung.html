Guide

Counting happens after the game: the cards one team has won are laid one by one onto a pile in front of the camera. The app recognises the top card each time and adds it up.

A count

  1. Tap Start counting.
  2. Choose deck, discipline, last trick and factor – every team knows them after the game – and tap Start counting again.
  3. Lay the cards on top of the pile one after the other. The app confirms every counted card with a short sound and a vibration. Sound and vibration can be adjusted or turned off in the settings under “Feedback”.
  4. Your points are on the left, the opponents' on the right. Done ends the count, and the result stays on the home screen until the next count.

Correcting

  • Recognised wrongly? Tap the card in the list at the bottom – it is removed from the count.
  • Card missing? This button adds a card by hand, say one that lies very flat.
  • Reset empties the pile and starts over.

The cards the app knows

  • French: the Swiss Jass cards with French suits by AGMüller (Neuhausen) – Clubs, Diamonds, Hearts, Spades.
  • German: the Schaffhausen playing cards “Opti-Quatro” by AGMüller – Acorns, Bells, Roses, Shields.

Other designs – even from other makers with the same suits – are not recognised reliably. Do you play with another deck? Get in touch, with photos of the 36 cards the recognition can be trained on it.
