Write to us

Questions, bugs, a deck the app does not know yet: an email to support@yarx.ch is all it takes. The version of the app (under About in the app) and the device help.

For a new set of cards: photos of all 36 cards, one by one, flat from above and in good light. They are used only to train the recognition and are not published.
