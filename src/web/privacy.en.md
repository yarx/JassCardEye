# Privacy policy JassCardEye

*As of 14 September 2026*

Published at https://jasscardeye.yarx.ch/en/datenschutz (`src/web/src/app/pages/en/datenschutz.html`).
Both say the same, and so do the other languages - whoever changes one changes all of them.

## In short

JassCardEye collects no data. The camera images are analysed on the device and discarded at once,
the app has no network connection of its own, there is no user account and no analytics or
advertising service. On its own, the app stores only its settings.

## Responsible

YARX GmbH, Wolfgrubenstrasse 56, 5742 Kölliken, Switzerland. Contact: support@yarx.ch

## Camera

The app needs access to the camera to recognise the top card on a pile of Jass cards. The images are
processed on the device only, by a model that is part of the app. No image leaves the device, and on
its own the app stores none. Access is asked for the first time the counting screen opens and can be
revoked at any time in the system settings.

## What is stored on the device

On its own, the app stores only settings, such as the deck chosen last, lens, stability and rule,
vibration and sound. They are kept in the app settings of the device and serve only to have the app
set up the same way the next time it starts. Deleting the app removes them. On Android the app takes
no part in the backup to a Google account.

## Purchase

The counted points can be unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase runs entirely
through Apple or Google: the app receives neither payment nor account data. It loads only the price
from the App Store or Google Play and asks whether the purchase exists. The privacy policies of
Apple and Google apply to this.

## What does not happen

- No transfer of camera images or usage data to us or to third parties
- No user account, no sign-in, no registration
- No analytics, tracking or advertising services, no cookies, no advertising identifier
- No sharing or selling of data
- No access to contacts, location, photo library or microphone

## App Store, TestFlight and Google Play

If the app is obtained through the App Store or TestFlight, Apple collects data of its own, such as
crash reports and usage statistics, if sharing with app developers is allowed, and with TestFlight
the feedback testers send. If the app is obtained or tested through Google Play, Google collects
data of its own, such as crash and performance data of the devices. The privacy policies of Apple
and Google apply to this. From it we receive only summarised reports and the feedback testers
deliberately send.

## This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no embedded third-party services. It is delivered by
Microsoft Azure Static Web Apps; for technical reasons this produces server logs (such as IP address
and time of access), which Microsoft processes to operate and secure the service.

## Your rights

As we collect no personal data, we hold none that could be disclosed or deleted. For questions, an
email to support@yarx.ch is all it takes.

## Changes

If anything about the processing of data changes, this page is updated and the date at the top with
